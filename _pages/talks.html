Conference proceedings, primarily at top-tier IEEE and IFAC control venues (CDC, ACC, ECC, IFAC World Congress). Regular TPC member for IEEE conferences.

Selected Proceedings

Control & System Identification

  • Low-order model identification of MIMO systems from noisy and incomplete data. IEEE CDC, 2015.
  • Parsimonious model identification via atomic norm minimization. European Control Conference (ECC), 2014.
  • Recursive parsimonious system identification algorithm for dynamical systems. IEEE CCTA, 2018.
  • System identification algorithm for non-uniformly sampled data. IFAC World Congress, 2017.
  • Parsimonious Volterra system identification. American Control Conference (ACC), 2018.
  • A robust MPC approach to the design of behavioural treatments. ACC, 2018.

Robotics & Vision-Based Control

  • Vision based control of an autonomous blimp with actuator saturation using pulse-width modulation. IEEE CCA, 2013.

Estimation, Tracking & Anomaly Detection

  • An efficient approach to the radar ghost elimination problem. ACC, 2018.
  • Hankel-based unsupervised anomaly detection. ACC, 2020.

Applied MPC, IoT & Building Systems

  • An Internet of Things compliant model identification methodology for smart buildings. IEEE CDC, 2017.
  • A scalable contract based approach for integrating building flexibility to energy grids. ECC, 2019.
